## Deviations

- **The frontmatter is the fork's.** The description names the prose the
  skill applies to, and the model may invoke the skill on its own writing.
- **The head is the fork's.** Upstream aims at prose that passes as
  written by a person. This copy aims at prose a reviewer or another agent
  can skim: say the fact in the fewest words. A paragraph after the
  process says why.
- **The pattern list is upstream's**, as it stood in September 2026.
